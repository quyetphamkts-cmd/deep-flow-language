# PROJECT BRIEF — Deep Flow Language / English Listening Curriculum
Version: 1.0 | 2026-10-28 | Status: approved direction, NOT finished curriculum

## Purpose
Create an original English listening-first curriculum, suitable for repeated listening, listening comprehension, natural rapid responses, and meaningful long-term retention. User's app: Deep Flow Language. Do not confuse this educational content initiative with existing production code or assume repository access.

## Scope / approved
- 12 connected core story lessons: **The Architect’s Journey**, working protagonist David, a Vietnamese architect and father. One overarching narrative, working theme **The House of Tomorrow**. Core story titles are proposals, not locked final scripts.
- 60 optional, independently comprehensible expansion adventures: **The Twelve Zodiac Adventures**, five adventures for each Vietnamese zodiac animal: Mouse, Buffalo, Tiger, Cat (NOT rabbit), Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. Inspired by Vietnamese folklore and culture, with wholly original writing and dialogue. 12 groups aligned to core lessons C01–C12 for vocabulary review, without requiring expansions for progression.
- 3,000 **distinct target common English vocabulary entries** across core curriculum; provisional allocation 250 newly targeted entries per core lesson. Vocabulary list, source license, word normalization (lemma vs inflection), coverage and feasibility NOT yet validated. Prioritize easy, natural listening over forced lexical stuffing. A word being mentioned is NOT proof of learner mastery.
- Core audio **30 minutes preferred, up to 45 minutes if needed**, TOTAL across all four sections; 12 × 30–45 = 6–9 hours.
- Expansion audio ~15 minutes TOTAL across two sections; 60 × 15 = 15 hours. Combined first-pass audio 21–24 hours.
- Three optional pacing plans: 3, 6, or 12 months; same content, different repetition schedule. Expansions are optional, support audio / synchronized read+listen / read-only.

## Core audio sections
1. Main Story: short, clear original story with vivid memorable scenes and naturally contextualized target vocabulary.
2. Word Discovery: explain **only selected words empirically likely to be difficult to retain**, using familiarity, abstractness, polysemy, pronunciation/spelling mismatch, context inferability and later learner performance. Do NOT explain all 250 target words. Typical 10–20 candidates is a hypothesis, not a fixed quota.
3. Quick Response: dominant practice time, many simple questions, explicit learner-answer pauses and spoken model answers; varied repetition, gradual difficulty, active recall.
4. Time Shift: retell portions from different times / points of view; natural grammar exposure, not formal rule lectures.
Provisional 30-min allocation: 5/5/15/5. Provisional 45-min allocation: 10/5/22/8. Actual durations must be measured with generated audio including pauses; redistribute as content requires.

## Expansion audio sections
- Daily Adventure: original zodiac adventure and natural dialogue (~5 min).
- Quick Response: responsive Q&A (~10 min).
Independent story, realistic transferable conversational phrases even in fantastic settings. Reviews current and earlier core vocabulary with spaced practice. No rigid lexical ratios; adapt to learning history and story coherence.

## Learning and storytelling standards
- Listen → Understand → Respond → Repeat; listening and question-response practice prioritized over translation or rote memorization.
- Pixar-inspired STORY CRAFT principles (not copied scripts): compelling flawed character, clear desire, escalating obstacles, consequences, emotional stakes, visual surprise with causal coherence, meaningful choice and transformation, concise storytelling; plan ending and character change.
- Memory-informed writing: imaginative imagery, surprise, humor, emotional relevance, linked keyword images, contrast, logical and chronological grouping, recurring motifs, spaced review and active retrieval. Treat these as design hypotheses; test with learners. Avoid making unscientific claims about cultural groups or universal memory techniques.
- Keep sentences simple and natural, progressive A1–B1 accessibility, suitable for repeated listening. Content should be genuinely engaging to both adults and children without excessive complexity.
- Core series: one continuous arc; expansions: self-contained stories within a shared zodiac world. Use culturally respectful Vietnamese references and accurate distinctions (Mão = Cat).

## Rights / originality
- Use pedagogical structures inspired by Effortless English, not its copyrighted scripts, recordings, trademarks or proprietary course assets. Naming is original.
- Traditional Vietnamese folklore and zodiac motifs can inspire original plots. Check provenance and attribution requirements for folk sources. Never copy modern retellings, illustrations, music, narration, film characters, or specific creative expression without rights. Confirm licensing of vocabulary source, voice, fonts and media before commercial distribution. This brief is not legal clearance.

## Quality gates
1. Narrative continuity (core) and standalone intelligibility (expansions).
2. Originality/IP review and cultural accuracy.
3. Target-word inventory integrity: exactly 3,000 unique normalized entries once source finalized; explicit assignment, coverage, repetitions and out-of-list counts.
4. Listening clarity and meaningful Q&A with pauses and correct answers.
5. Word Discovery chosen by defensible difficulty criteria, not exhaustive glossing.
6. Actual synthesized audio length per section and total; 30–45 min core, ~15 min expansion.
7. Spaced-review logic with no dependency on optional expansions.
8. Human editorial review before publication.
